# Meeting prep

For each external attendee:
1. `recall` the person and their company.
2. `gmail_search` their address for the last few threads; summarize the relationship and anything open.
3. If they're new or memory is thin: `web_search` the person + company (role, recent news, funding).
   Save what you learn with `remember` (kind `person`).

Then write:
```
*14:00 — Acme intro* (45 min, Zoom)
Who: Priya Shah, CTO Acme (Series B, 80 people). Met at Web Summit.
Why: they asked about the API partnership (her email 3 Oct).
Open: you promised pricing by end of month.
Ask: integration timeline, who signs off.
```
Keep each meeting under 6 lines.
